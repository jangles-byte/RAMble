# Murmuration

*Design philosophy for the Murmuration scene — boids flocking as "the
system's nerves." Built on the Atelier generative-motion system reference:
separation/alignment/cohesion with a clamped speed band, wrapped edges,
spatial hash.*

**The job:** make system *pressure* visceral. A calm machine is a calm
flock; a stressed machine is a flock in fear. The viewer reads the mood
before they read any detail.

Murmuration believes emergence is the point: no bird knows the shape, yet
the shape exists. Hundreds of streaked birds wheel through the dark in one
connected organism whose knots and voids are the composition — density is
data, emptiness is deliberate.

Memory is population: the flock grows as RAM fills. Stress is fear — under
pressure, separation overpowers alignment, the speed band rises, and the
smooth murmuration frays into nervous scatter; when pressure releases, the
flock re-forms, and that re-forming is the most beautiful moment the scene
has. Swap summons a predator: a red presence that roams the field and tears
the flock open wherever it goes — unmistakable, because swap should be.

Color is earned by crowd and by computation. Birds are ash-silver streaks;
where the flock knots, the knots glow brighter — neighbor density, not hue,
carries the light. Tokens fly as leaders: while a model streams, single
birds ignite in the theme's palette and streak through the flock, one per
token, dragging brief color through silver.

**Is not:** confetti, a bouncing screensaver, or evenly spread. Kills on
sight: rainbow-by-heading over the whole flock, edge bouncing (edges wrap),
a single blob orbiting the center.
**Signature move:** swap pressure is a visible red predator that hunts the
flock — the one thing users will describe to each other.
