# Arterial

*Design philosophy for the Synapse scene. Written before the ground-up rework;
every Synapse decision resolves against this document.*

**The job:** make "is the machine thinking, and how hard?" legible in half a
second of peripheral vision, from across the room, on any wallpaper.

Synapse believes thought is *directional flow*, not random twinkle. A mind
computing looks like current moving through structure — so the graph has an
artery: a trunk of hubs crossing the screen left to right, with dendrite
branches reaching up and down into the empty field. The default "neural
network" visual — a uniform scatter of linked dots — is what this design
exists to reject.

Space is organized around that artery. The trunk owns the horizontal middle
band; dendrites thin as they reach toward the screen edges, leaving deliberate
dark at the top and bottom corners. Density says "center of thought here";
emptiness is what makes the structure readable at a glance.

And a mind is *dense*. The artery does not cross a void — it runs through a
living canopy: a rich weave of branches and twigs in the mid-ground, a far
layer of ghost neurons breathing slowly in the fog behind it, and a faint
drift of synaptic dust in between. Depth is the richness axis: the trunk
blazes nearest the viewer, the canopy fills the middle, the ghost web recedes.
Three layers, one structure — full, never cluttered.

Color is computation — that is the whole palette rule. At rest the network is
ash-silver: fibers, nodes, everything near-achromatic, a quiet nervous system
under skin. Color exists only in moving signal: token pulses and cascade
flares carry the theme's palette, and a firing node blooms into its color for
a moment before cooling back to silver. An idle Mac shows a silver web; a
streaming LLM paints it. The harder it thinks, the more color the screen holds.

Motion temperament: purposeful, never fidgety. The resting state breathes —
a slow shimmer travels the length of the trunk like a pulse under skin, the
ghost layer swells and dims, occasional single firings mark the heartbeat.
Under inference the motion is *traffic*, not noise: comet-tailed pulses enter
the trunk at the left edge, race the full width, and shed cascades into the
branches as they pass each hub; when a cascade reaches a twig's end it bursts
into a handful of embers that fly free and die. Stress warms the palette and
quickens everything, but direction is never lost; even a storm flows left to
right.

**Is not:** a constellation, a screensaver, or symmetrical. Kills on sight:
uniform node scatter, random twinkle without cause, color present while the
machine is idle.

**Signature move:** every token the model generates is one pulse of color
that enters at the left and crosses the entire screen along the trunk —
idle, the web is silver; color only exists while the machine computes.
